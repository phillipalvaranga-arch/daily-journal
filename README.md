# My Daily Journal

An installable daily to-do journal. Add tasks, cross off what you finish, write a short journal for the day, and see your daily, weekly and monthly achievements and growth.

Live: https://phillipalvaranga-arch.github.io/daily-journal/

All of your tasks, notes and stats stay on this device. There is no account and nothing is sent to a server. Use Export and Import to make a backup or move your journal to another device.

Dates and times are shown in Central Time.

The product information and numbers in Achievements and Growth are computed only from the tasks you have entered.
